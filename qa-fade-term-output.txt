P5. gauge structure: what each form pins (delay-origin gauge, per arm)
====================================================================================================
the measured delays carry an unknown origin offset d (stored = true + d); the
parameters are only identifiable up to the gauge it induces:

  form gauge  identifiable combinations (7 params = combos + gauge freedoms)
  H      2  N,  A s,  W - N_m s_m - N_f s_f            (pins the product A s, not A or s)
  E      2  N,  s,  W + N_m s_m ln A_m + N_f s_f ln A_f  (pins the scale s; A via ln)
  L      0  W, N, A, s  --  the form is rigid: no origin-shift symmetry, all pinned
  T      2  N,  A/s,  W - N_m s_m - N_f s_f              (pins the absorption rate A/s)
  Q      2  N,  A/s^2,  W - N_m s_m - N_f s_f             (pins the initial curvature)

P6. verdict (measured)
====================================================================================================
shape effect only: delta-SSR against the FRESH H refit (removes the stored fit's drift
along the flat direction; negative = better than H):
  H: +0.00  E: -6.71  L: +7.67  T: +0.38  Q: +2.98  P: -5.10

per-group best candidate:  H:0  E:1  L:2  T:1  Q:1  P:7

the stored H fit itself sits +626.19 sig^2 above the fresh H optimum
(median over the 12 groups; max +54970.17 in the degenerate rosi group), so
the large gaps in P2 are the stored fit's drift along the flat direction, not the shape.

ranking by mean shape delta (negative = better than the fresh H fit):
  E: -6.71
  P: -5.10  (step +1.61)
  H: +0.00  (step +5.10)
  T: +0.38  (step +0.38)
  Q: +2.98  (step +2.61)
  L: +7.67  (step +4.69)

  verdict: E (the exponential) is the most suitable fade term. It is the best mean
  delta among the 2-parameter forms, never the worst group, is smooth, has a finite total
  absorbed cost (A s), and pins its scale s as a gauge invariant (P5). Its claim -- the
  visible cost is down to e^-5 ~ 0.7 % of A by u = 5 -- is falsifiable, unlike H's eternal
  1/m tail, whose total absorbed cost diverges. L is rejected (worst mean delta): the data
  see a linear initial absorption, F'(0) = -A/s != 0. P wins the most per-group contests, but
  its mean is worse than E's and its extra (s, k) is unresolvable (k hits the 0.2-10 bound,
  1sig up to 3512 % in P3): those wins are the extra parameter, not an identified shape.
  T ties H on average and is the pure-overlap physical null model (hard cutoff at the
  per-call slack); keep it as the benchmark -- runs with delay > s showing exactly zero fade
  would confirm T, a residual ~ A e^(-m/s) would confirm E. H stays defensible (middle of
  the pack) but is not the best; the next fit round should use E, re-anchored on the fresh
  optimum, not on the stored fit (which sits hundreds of sig^2 above the H optimum).
